Write one document with role sample-questions: an exam-style practice set with model solutions.

Cover the major topics and every attached source with usable material. Give each topic a \section* that names its file and cites the pages with \src.

Design questions a student can attempt from the material provided. Mix retrieval, explanation, proof and application according to the subject, from routine to challenging, and include a question that asks the student to choose or justify a method where that is a learning objective. Use self-contained givens for calculations. Phrase invented scenarios as hypothetical. For a figure-based question, identify the figure or reproduce its necessary legible values.

Put each question in its own box, labelled Q1, Q2, and so on continuously across topics, with its kind after the label: \begin{question}{Q3 · Prove} ... \end{question} (kinds such as Recall, Explain, Compute, Prove, Apply, Challenge). Keep solutions out of the question text; a short hint may follow in italics only for Challenge questions.

After the last question, add \section*{Answers}. Follow it with one answer box per question, in the same order, labelled with the same Q number: \begin{answer}{Q3} ... \end{answer}. Give the result with the decisive steps or explanation, as a model solution a grader would accept, and add a pitfall box after an answer when students commonly go wrong there. Check that each answer follows from the givens in its own question.
